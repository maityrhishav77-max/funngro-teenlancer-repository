FAQ

Questions teen earners ask most.

Is Funngro free to join?+

Yes. Funngro is free, forever — there's no cost to create a profile or apply to campaigns.

What is Gig Score (GS)?+

Your Gig Score is a simple reputation number that grows as you complete your profile and deliver campaigns. A fuller profile and reliable delivery raise your GS, and a higher GS surfaces you for better-paying campaigns.

How do I get paid?+

Payments go straight to your UPI ID once a brand approves your submission — no invoices or bank transfers to chase.

Do I need experience to start?+

No. Many earners start with product sampling or short reviews and move into content creation and paid promotions as their Gig Score grows.
